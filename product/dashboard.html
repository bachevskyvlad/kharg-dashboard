Fragility Index

Where current storage stands vs. the last 52 weeks. Low storage = high fragility (less buffer for disruptions).

Storage Drops (>1mn bbl / 2 weeks)→ Price -1.1% (4w), -1.9% (8w)
Storage Builds (>1mn bbl / 2 weeks)→ Price +2.5% (4w), +4.6% (8w)

Based on 51 events each (2019-2026). Storage is a lagging indicator.

Methodology

Source: Sentinel-1 SAR radar (ESA). Works through clouds.

Coverage: 134 floating-roof tanks across 7 terminals in Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait.

Limitations: Export terminals only. ~6-day satellite revisit.
