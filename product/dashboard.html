Export Coverage Ratio --
Export Velocity ÷ Production Inflow · per pass
Export Coverage Ratio = Export Velocity ÷ Production Inflow. Shows whether exported oil comes from current field production or from accumulated strategic reserves.

ECR > 1.2 (green) — Iran is exporting more than fields are delivering. Terminal is actively drawing down strategic reserves. Historically coincides with price pressure or urgent sales.

ECR ≈ 1 (grey, 0.85–1.2) — pass-through mode: production flows directly to export with minimal inventory change.

ECR < 0.85 (red) — production outpaces exports, inventories accumulating. Signal of export constraints: sanctions pressure on buyers, tanker shortage, or deliberate strategic accumulation ahead of an expected price move.

Export Velocity & Production Inflow
Mb/d per SAR pass · export below zero · inflow above zero
Export Velocity (green, below zero) — rate of oil leaving the terminal to tankers, Mb/d. Computed as the total volume decrease across all draining tanks per SAR pass, normalised by days between observations. High export = active loading. Low = shipments paused or slowing.

Production Inflow (red, above zero) — rate of oil arriving from oilfields into storage, Mb/d. Computed as total volume increase across all filling tanks. Proxy for production rate: stable inflow = steady field output. Sharp drop = production halt or inter-terminal transfer. Sharp rise = new production stream or return from idle.

Key limitation: SAR cannot distinguish internal tank-to-tank transfers from real exports or field production. Large events (>1.5 Mb per pass) are very likely real operations. Small movements may be operational noise.
