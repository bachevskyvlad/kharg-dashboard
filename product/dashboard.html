Methodology & Definitions

How to Read This Dashboard

Start with Block 1 — current storage level and direction. If SPI >85%, a price discount or forced export is historically likely within 4–8 weeks.

Block 2 — check when the last export pulse was and how many have happened. The interval estimate predicts the next probable loading window.

Block 3 — the current regime (BUILD vs DRAWDOWN) tells you what the terminal is doing right now. Monthly balance shows seasonal rhythm.

Block 4 — per-tank detail. Active tanks: t60_2, t55_17 (high σ). Probable strategic reserve: t55_31, t60_1 (chronically full).

Data & Methodology

Source: Sentinel-1 C-band SAR (ESA Copernicus). Penetrates clouds, 24/7 coverage.

Signal: Inverse crescent radiometry — radar shadow on the far side of each floating-roof tank. Lower VV backscatter (dB) = more oil.

Volume: V = π × r² × h_fill × 6.29 bbl/m³. Fill ratio from per-tank dB calibrated to 5th/95th percentile range across all observations.

Coverage: 47 tanks at Kharg Island (~90% of Iranian crude exports). Max theoretical capacity: 33.2 Mb.

Revisit: ~6 days average. Limitations: export terminal only; heavy rain can affect signal quality.
