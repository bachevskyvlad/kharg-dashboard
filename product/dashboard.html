Metric Definitions

Fill Rate: (vol[t] − vol[t−7]) / 7 days. Positive = Iran accumulating. Negative = active loading.

Storage Pressure Index: vol / max_capacity. Above 85% historically precedes price concessions or forced exports.

Export Pulse Score: Triggered when storage drops >2 Mb over 6–12 days. Probable VLCC tanker loading event, 1–2 weeks ahead of AIS confirmation.

Structural Idle Ratio: % of tanks with 90-day mean backscatter >12 dB — consistently empty or non-operational.

Volatility Spike Alert: Date where cross-tank σ exceeds historical mean + 1.5σ — signals abnormal simultaneous activity across multiple tanks.

Monthly Balance: Net storage change per calendar month. Persistent negative months = sustained export activity.

Export Interval: Average days between consecutive VLCC loading events — proxy for terminal throughput tempo.

Methodology

Source: Sentinel-1 C-band SAR (ESA Copernicus). Penetrates clouds and darkness.

Coverage: 47 floating-roof tanks at Kharg Island (~90% of Iranian crude exports).

Signal: Inverse crescent radiometry — radar shadow on the far side of each tank reveals fill level. Lower VV backscatter (dB) = higher oil level.

Volume formula: V = π × r² × h_current × 6.29 bbl/m³. Fill ratio derived from per-tank dB calibrated to 5th/95th percentile range.

Revisit: ~6 days average, sometimes 2–3 times/week on overlapping passes.

Limitations: Export terminal only. Rain and strong wind can affect SAR signal quality.
