Methodology

🛰️ How The Radar Works

Kharg Island tanks use floating roofs that lower as oil is pumped out and rise as tanks fill. Sentinel-1 SAR signals bounce off the internal walls. A low roof returns a brighter radar reflection. We invert this radiometric signal to calculate physical volume in millions of barrels.

On average, the satellite passes over the region every ~6 days.

📊 What BUILD / DRAWDOWN Means

A BUILDING signal means crude is accumulating at Kharg — exports are constrained or demand from buyers is soft. A DRAWDOWN signal means tankers are loading — Iranian crude is entering the global market.

⏱️ Lead Over EIA & JODI

Official agencies publish storage data with a 4–7 week lag. SAR-derived signals from Kharg are available within days of each satellite pass — giving traders and analysts a meaningful head start on Iranian crude supply dynamics.
