About
Vlad Bachevsky
Founder & Analyst

OilRadar is built and run by one person. SAR signal processing, pipeline engineering, market interpretation — all in-house. No committee, no consensus view.

The focus is narrow by design: 47 floating-roof tanks at Kharg Island, Iran's primary crude export terminal. Sentinel-1 radar passes weekly. The signal arrives 4–7 weeks before EIA or JODI publish their numbers.

The methodology is physical — inverse crescent radiometry on SAR backscatter. Not survey-based. Not AIS-dependent. The tanks don't lie.
