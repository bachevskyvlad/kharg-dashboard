How It Works

🛰️ SAR Backscatter Inversion

Sentinel-1 synthetic aperture radar transmits microwave pulses and measures the reflected signal. Each floating-roof tank returns a distinctive crescent-shaped reflection at the internal tank wall. When the roof sits low (empty tank), the exposed wall is taller and returns a brighter signal. We invert this — inverse crescent radiometry — to extract fill level.

📐 Volume Calculation

Fill ratio is derived from per-tank SAR backscatter (dB mean) calibrated against each tank's 5th–95th percentile historical range. Volume in Mb = π × r² × H_effective × 6.2898 bbl/m³, where H_effective = H_min + fill_ratio × (H_max − H_min).

🔒 Data Latency

Each satellite pass produces one observation per tank per overpass. The latest 2 weeks of data are gated for subscribers to prevent front-running of the signal before it enters public domain. The live tank map is available via the full intelligence feed.
