The Process

From pasture to parlour

Six steps, no shortcuts. Everything we cannot say happens by hand, does not happen at all.

1

Selection

One herd, one pasture, one season per batch.

2

Curing

4–6 months open-air, turned weekly.

3

Blending

Folded with hand-cut hay, by hand.

4

Pressing

5

Questions, Answered Honestly

Before you reserve

Is this actually manure and hay?

Yes. Precisely that, aged, dried, hand-pressed and sealed. We are not going to pretend otherwise — we simply think it deserves better packaging than a feed sack.

Does it smell while burning?

A faint, warm, hay-and-woodsmoke note in the first minutes, then very little. Several customers describe it as "barn-adjacent, in a nice way."

Why does a brick of dung cost more than firewood?

Because firewood does not come with the name of the cow that made it possible. You are paying for provenance, packaging, and a very good story to tell at dinner.

Is it safe for indoor fireplaces and wood stoves?

Yes — properly cured dung fuel has been used in hearths across Europe and Asia for centuries. Burn as you would any dense biomass log, with adequate ventilation.

Can I really trace my exact brick?

Every brick is stamped with a serial at the press. Enter it in the Provenance Registry above and we'll surface its herd, pasture, herder, and press date.
