Detection method

Each SAR image (Sentinel-1 IW mode, VV polarisation, 35 m/px) is processed with an adaptive backscatter threshold calibrated to local sea clutter. Compact bright objects above threshold are classified as vessels; length is estimated from the bounding box of each detected blob.

Land and coastal clutter are masked using the Natural Earth 10 m coastline with an ~875 m sea buffer. The Bosphorus transit channel is excluded — only the Black Sea waiting anchorage is counted.

Queue index

Queue Index = (vessels on pass date) ÷ (10-year average for that calendar month) × 100. A value of 100 is exactly average for the season. Vessel classes follow standard maritime size conventions by detected length.

Capesize ≥ 290 m · Post-Panamax ≥ 260 m · Panamax ≥ 220 m · Handymax ≥ 100 m. Vessels under 100 m (coastal and small craft) are excluded from the count.
