🛰️ How The Radar Works

Oil storage tanks on Kharg Island use floating roofs that lower as oil is pumped out and rise as tanks fill. Sentinel-1 SAR signals bounce off the internal walls. A low roof returns a brighter radar reflection. We invert this radiometric signal to calculate the physical volume in millions of barrels.

On average, the satellite passes over the region every ~6 days.

Supply Chain Context

Crude loaded at Kharg Island flows east through the Strait of Malacca — the primary chokepoint for Persian Gulf oil moving to Asia. The majority of Iranian crude transiting Malacca is bound for Shandong province refineries (山东炼厂), China's largest cluster of independent teapot refiners. A drawdown at Kharg typically precedes a Malacca transit 10–18 days later and a Shandong delivery 25–35 days after loading.
