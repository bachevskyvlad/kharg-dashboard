🛰️ How The Radar Works

Oil storage tanks on Kharg Island use floating roofs that lower as oil is pumped out and rise as tanks fill. Sentinel-1 SAR signals bounce off the internal walls. A low roof returns a brighter radar reflection. We invert this radiometric signal to calculate the physical volume in millions of barrels.

On average, the satellite passes over the region twice a week.

📊 Reading The Signal

Rising Volume (Build): Oil is accumulating. Tankers are likely delayed or loading is slower than production.

Falling Volume (Drawdown): Tankers are draining the terminal, sending physical supply into the global market.
