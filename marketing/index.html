Methodology

How The
Signal Is Built

🛰️

SAR Acquisition

Sentinel-1 C-band radar (5.6 GHz) images Kharg Island 2–3 times per week on descending passes. Works through clouds, at night, in all weather.

🔍

Crescent Mask

A 15-meter far-side crescent mask is applied to each tank. The radar shadow on the illuminated far side reveals the float height — and therefore, the oil level.

📐

Volume Calc

Fill ratio × tank geometry (known radius + max height from API 650) = oil volume in barrels. Summed across all 47 tanks to get total terminal storage.

📊

Export Signal

Storage build = tanker queuing or export friction. Storage draw = active loading. This physical signal precedes AIS tracking by 7–14 days and EIA by 4–7 weeks.

Fill Rate

Accumulation Speed

→ Trade signal: negative fill rate precedes tanker arrival by 7–14 days

Storage Pressure Index

Forced Sell Risk

vol_current / 33.2 Mn bbl × 100

→ Alert threshold: SPI >85% — watch for price discount or export surge

Export Pulse Score

Drain >2 Mn bbl in 6–12 days
→ Probable tanker loading

Detects rapid storage drawdowns consistent with one or more VLCC tankers loading at berth. Each event is a probable export pulse 1–2 weeks before AIS confirmation.

→ Count of events last 90 days with start/end dates displayed in app

Infrastructure Health

Tanks with 90d mean dB > 12
→ % of 47 total tanks

Identifies tanks that are chronically empty or offline — possibly under maintenance, decommissioned, or used for non-crude products.
